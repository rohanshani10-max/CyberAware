Understanding Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity helps protect our digital devices, online accounts, networks and personal information.

🔐

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity refers to the practices used to protect computers, networks, devices and digital information from unauthorized access and cyber threats.

🌐

Why is it Important?

It helps protect personal information, online accounts and financial details while using digital services.

BE AWARE

Common Online Scams

Learn how cybercriminals trick people and understand how to stay protected.

🏦

Fake Bank KYC Scam

Fraudsters send fake bank messages asking users to update their KYC through suspicious links.

Safety:

Use only your bank's official website or app.

💳

UPI Payment Scam

Scammers send fake payment requests and trick users into entering their UPI PIN.

Safety:

Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

💼

Fake Job Scam

Fraudsters offer fake jobs and demand registration fees or personal information.

Safety:

Verify employers before sharing information or paying fees.

STAY PROTECTED

Essential Safety Tips

Follow these simple practices to keep your personal information and online accounts safe.

🔑

Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords for different online accounts. Avoid sharing them.

🔐

Enable 2FA

Enable two-factor authentication to add an extra layer of security to your accounts.

📩

Avoid Suspicious Links

Never click unknown links or download attachments from suspicious messages.

📶

Secure Wi-Fi

Avoid accessing sensitive accounts through unsecured public Wi-Fi networks.

🔄

Update Your Devices

Keep your operating system, browser and applications updated regularly.

🚨

Report Cybercrime

Report suspected cyber fraud through official cybercrime reporting channels.

TAKE ACTION

What to Do If You Get Scammed?

Act quickly to reduce the risk of further financial loss.

01. Contact Your Bank

Immediately contact your bank or payment provider and report the suspicious transaction.

02. Call 1930

For financial cyber fraud in India, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 as soon as possible.

03. Report Online

Submit a complaint through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

04. Save Evidence

Keep screenshots, transaction IDs, messages and other relevant evidence.
